Stability, not activity, is the default outcome of sufficient constraint.

A configuration exceeds tolerance limits

External perturbations destabilize geometry

Internal symmetry conditions are violated

Dimension W governs these tolerance thresholds by defining which geometries are permitted to persist.

Nuclear fission and fusion are commonly described in terms of binding energy and mass defects. These descriptions remain accurate, but they obscure the deeper geometric reality.

In both cases:

Energy released = reduction in constraint density

Nothing is destroyed. Geometry is reorganized.

Nuclear systems sit at the extreme end of constraint density.

Consciousness performs this role.
